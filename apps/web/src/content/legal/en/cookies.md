## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser so that it works or to remember information about your visit. This policy applies in accordance with article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) and the guidance of the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

## 2. Brand website ({{website}})

The brand website uses one technical cookie, `zm_consent`, to remember your cookie choice. Only if you accept it in the cookie notice does it also use **Google Analytics**, to measure in aggregate how many visits it receives and how it is used, and, if you also accept advertising, to measure our ads on Google. You can change your choice at any time from “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people apart from automated submissions. Turnstile works without tracking cookies (<span class="todo">TODO: confirm with the final Cloudflare configuration</span>).

## 3. Online shop ({{shopWebsite}})

The shop uses **technical cookies**, which are essential for it to work: keeping your cart, your signed-in session and your language, and remembering your cookie choice. As they are strictly necessary, they do not require your consent. As on the website, Google Analytics and advertising are only enabled if you accept them in the cookie notice.

| Cookie         | Owner                | Purpose                                                  | Duration                                                        |
| -------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `PrestaShop-…` | Zement (first party) | Keep your cart, signed-in session, language and currency | 20 days                                                         |
| `PHPSESSID`    | Zement (first party) | Keep the technical session while you browse              | <span class="todo">TODO: set to browser session on IONOS</span> |
| `zm_consent`   | Zement (first party) | Remember your cookie choice (website and shop)           | 6 months                                                        |

**Third-party cookies, only with your consent** (website and shop):

| Cookie     | Owner                | Purpose                                           | Duration |
| ---------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| `_ga`      | Google (third party) | Google Analytics: distinguish visits anonymously  | 2 years  |
| `_ga_<id>` | Google (third party) | Google Analytics: keep the state of the visit     | 2 years  |
| `_gcl_au`  | Google (third party) | Advertising: measure the effectiveness of our ads | 90 days  |

## 4. How to manage or delete cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the shop’s technical cookies, you may not be able to keep your cart or sign in.

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge)
