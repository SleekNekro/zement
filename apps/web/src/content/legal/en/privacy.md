## 2. What data we process, why and on what legal basis

### Contact form and quote requests

- **Data:** name, email, phone (optional), project postcode and area (optional), the interest you select and your message.
- **Purpose:** to answer your enquiry, prepare an application quote or inform you about a professional account.
- **Legal basis:** taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR) and, otherwise, your consent when you submit the form (art. 6.1.a GDPR).
- **Retention:** as long as needed to handle the enquiry and, if no contract follows, for a maximum of <span class="todo">TODO: period (e.g. 12 months)</span>.

### Customer account and professional account in the shop

- **Data:** name, email, password (encrypted), addresses and, for professional accounts, company name and tax ID.
- **Purpose:** to manage your account, verify professional status and apply your price list.
- **Legal basis:** the contractual relationship (art. 6.1.b GDPR).
- **Retention:** while the account is active and, afterwards, for the limitation periods of any resulting claims.

### Orders

- **Data:** identification and contact details, shipping and billing addresses, products purchased and amounts. Card details are processed directly by the payment gateway; Zement neither sees nor stores them.
- **Purpose:** to process, ship and invoice orders and to handle returns and warranty claims.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR) and compliance with tax and commercial legal obligations (art. 6.1.c GDPR).
- **Retention:** for the periods required by law (6 years for commercial records, art. 30 of the Spanish Commercial Code; 4 years for tax records, art. 66 of the Spanish General Tax Law).

### Site security and abuse prevention

- **Data:** IP address and technical request data.
- **Purpose:** to protect the forms against automated submissions and to limit abusive attempts.
- **Legal basis:** Zement’s legitimate interest in the security of its systems (art. 6.1.f GDPR).
- **Retention:** the IP address is stored in encrypted (hashed) form for a maximum of 10 minutes to limit attempts.

We do not use your data for profiling and we do not make automated decisions that affect you. We do not send marketing communications without your prior consent.

## 4. Your rights

You may at any time exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability, and withdraw any consent given, by writing to {{email}} and stating which right you wish to exercise. We may ask you to verify your identity.

If you believe we have not handled your rights correctly, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency ([www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

## 5. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk: encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to data, encrypted passwords and protection of the forms against abuse.

## 6. Minors

Zement’s services are not aimed at children under 14. If you are under that age, please do not provide us with personal data.
